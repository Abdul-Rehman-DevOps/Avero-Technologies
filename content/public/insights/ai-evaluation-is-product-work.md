If you cannot measure assistant quality, you cannot improve it.

## Own the fixtures

Product and domain experts must maintain the evaluation cases that matter to customers.

## Gate promotions

Model and prompt changes should fail CI when quality or safety regressions appear.

## Keep humans in the loop

High-stakes flows need review paths and clear escalation, not only automated scores.
