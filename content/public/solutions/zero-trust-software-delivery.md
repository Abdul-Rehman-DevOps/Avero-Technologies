## Problem
CI/CD trusts developer machines and long-lived credentials, creating supply-chain risk.

## Outcome
Identity-based pipelines with signed artifacts and auditable promotions.

## Approach
Replace standing keys with OIDC, sign artifacts, and enforce admission policy at deploy time.
